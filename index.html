Got questions?

Before You Work With Us

Here's what founders and growth teams ask us most before working together.

How long does a full cycle take, from signal to creative?

＋

Most cycles run start to finish in about 2 weeks: 3-5 days for signal analysis and the strategic recommendation, and 3-5 days for the creative brief, script, and static direction. Once your account is connected, we're not starting from scratch each time, so this stays fast on every repeat cycle.

What do we need to provide to get started?

＋

Read access to your Meta ad account and some basic brand context (audience, positioning, tone, what you don't want to say). We handle the analysis and creative development from there.

Do we get a new recommendation every cycle, or do we choose what to test?

＋

Both work. We'll always surface our top recommendation with the reasoning behind it, but if you want to prioritize a specific angle or product, tell us and we'll build around it.

What happens after a test goes live?

＋

Once your team launches it and shares performance back with us, we fold those results into the next recommendation. This keeps every cycle sharper than the last, without us ever touching your ad account beyond the read access needed for analysis.

Can we run multiple cycles at once, for different products or campaigns?

＋

Yes. If you're managing multiple product lines or ad accounts, we can run parallel cycles rather than forcing everything through one queue.

What if the recommended test underperforms?

＋

That's real, useful signal, not a failure. We fold it directly into the next recommendation rather than treating it as a dead end. Part of the reasoning behind a recommendation is the tests that came before it.

Is there a minimum commitment, or can we do a single cycle to try it out?

＋

Reach out and we'll scope what makes sense. This isn't a bundled tier list on the site, since the right cadence depends on your ad spend and how many brands or accounts you're running.
